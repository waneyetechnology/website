ARCHIVE METHODOLOGY

Designed for accountability

Market analysis is most useful when its original assumptions remain visible. Waneye preserves timestamped reports independently from the live site so past conclusions can be examined against what followed.

The archive records executive highlights, sector signals, risk assessments, strategic recommendations, outlook and the indices of source headlines used by the analysis.
