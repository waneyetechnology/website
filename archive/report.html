05 / OUTLOOK

The road ahead

1—3 MONTHS

Elevated market volatility and defensive equity positioning over the next 1-3 months. The combination of $110 oil, accelerating producer price inflation, and AI credit strain will drive corporate margin compression and downward earnings revisions.

6—12 MONTHS

A 6-12 month rotation favoring cash-generative value, energy infrastructure, and select emerging market financial assets. Sustainable equity bottoming requires de-escalation of energy chokepoints and stabilization of sovereign debt refinancing costs.
